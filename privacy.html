Privacy Policy

This app is a personal publishing tool used only by its owner, Mina Tarek, to post his own content to his own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

It stores the access tokens of the owner's accounts on the owner's computer, and uses them only to publish the owner's posts and read the results of those posts. It collects no data from anyone else, and shares nothing with third parties.

The owner can revoke access at any time from the Instagram and TikTok app settings.
